01About

I build games and immersive applications in Unity — gameplay systems, multiplayer networking, and the optimization that keeps a build running smoothly on the target hardware.

I started out building casual and hyper-casual card games at Arclite Systems for the App Store and Google Play, then AR and VR experiences at Alienide Interactive, moved on to shipping features for a live casino-style title at Torpedo Labs, and now develop real-time XR systems at the University of Missouri on Meta Quest, HoloLens, Magic Leap and HTC Vive — work that has produced six peer-reviewed publications at top-tier XR venues.
