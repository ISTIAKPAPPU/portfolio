01About

I build real-time XR systems and games in Unity: headset applications that bring AI models into live sessions, gameplay systems and real-time multiplayer.

Today, as a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and a graduate research assistant in the DCPS Laboratory at the University of Missouri, I build real-time XR systems on Meta Quest, HoloLens, Magic Leap and HTC Vive; that research has produced 9 peer-reviewed papers and an IEEE ISMAR 2025 Best Poster Award. Before that, I started at Arclite Systems building casual and card games and shipped Air Hockey Glow HD to the App Store and Google Play (500K+ downloads and a 4.6-star rating on Google Play). At Softwind Tech I built AR and VR experiences for clients, from VR sports games and brand activations to a large-scale, multi-stage VR training simulation, and implemented real-time multiplayer with Photon. At Torpedo Labs I shipped player-facing features and game modes for the live casino-style title Wynn Slots across its Unity/C# client and Java backend.
