About

XR systems and research

XR Rubik's Cube Solving Assistant

XR research system on Magic Leap 2

Built a Magic Leap 2 assistant in Unity, C# and OpenXR that reads cube state from egocentric camera frames, queries a vision-language model for the next move, verifies each move before advancing, and guides the solve with 3D move previews, spatially anchored overlays and voice cues.

XR Cooking Assistant

XR research system on Magic Leap 2

Built a Magic Leap 2 assistant that reads ingredients, utensils and preparation progress from the headset camera and generates step-by-step vision-language-model guidance anchored to the relevant objects, with hazard cues for heat and sharp tools.

Adversarial VR

Open-source research testbed, IEEE ISMAR-Adjunct 2025

An open-source Unity testbed that streams live headset telemetry to cybersickness detection models and feeds responses back into the running session, enabling reproducible adversarial-robustness evaluation.

Research

My research studies how to make AI-powered immersive systems secure, private and comfortable: how manipulated AI guidance affects users in the headset, privacy-preserving XR methods, and cybersickness prediction and personalized mitigation. The work combines XR user studies with methods such as on-device gaze prediction that keeps raw eye-tracking data on the headset and explainable-AI-guided differential privacy.
